# Shared product vision (platform neutral)

**One product concept, two independently developed implementations.** Capture still photos or selected frames from recorded/live video of a stationary object with a handheld moving camera. Gradually reconstruct point clouds, mesh, and texture with trustworthy diagnostics. Possible future DAVID-style laser scanning and hybrid scanning are separate later modules, not v0.1.0 promises.

Both implementations respect `auxz2jz/master-instruction-library` and `CROSS_PLATFORM_COLLABORATION_STANDARD.md`. A shared feature does not imply identical implementation or engine. Never claim a capture preview is a 3D reconstruction.
